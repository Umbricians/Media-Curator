# Web asset role — what an image is for on a website. Media Curator's own scheme, licence-free.
# Codes have no spaces, dashes, underscores or dots, so a filename prefix such as "BRD1 acme logo.png"
# resolves with the filename code source. Top-level codes are deliberately not English words.
# Format: code|name|parentCode
BKG|Backgrounds|
BKG1|Background or texture|BKG
BKG2|Pattern|BKG
BRD|Branding|
BRD1|Logo|BRD
BRD2|Icon|BRD
BRD3|Favicon or app icon|BRD
EDT|Editorial|
EDT1|Article photo|EDT
EDT2|Illustration|EDT
EDT3|Stock or concept photo|EDT
INF|Information|
INF1|Chart or infographic|INF
INF2|Diagram|INF
INF3|Screenshot|INF
INF4|Map|INF
INF5|Document scan|INF
MKT|Marketing|
MKT1|Hero or banner|MKT
MKT2|Promotional graphic|MKT
MKT3|Social media image|MKT
PPL|People|
PPL1|Headshot or portrait|PPL
PPL2|Team or group photo|PPL
PPL3|Event photo|PPL
PRD|Products|
PRD1|Product shot|PRD
PRD2|Product in use|PRD
PRD3|Product detail|PRD
